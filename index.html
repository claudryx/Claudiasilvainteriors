About

My passion for interior design began with our home in Santander, Northern Spain, where I first discovered the joy of shaping spaces and creating an atmosphere that feels warm, thoughtful and personal.

Since then, that curiosity has grown through projects including our house in the Cotswolds. Each space has allowed me to explore colour, texture and craftsmanship, with the aim of designing homes that feel timeless, comfortable and quietly elegant.
