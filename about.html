About

My passion for creating beautiful homes began with our family home in Santander, Northern Spain, where I first discovered the joy of transforming spaces and creating rooms that feel warm, welcoming and deeply personal.

Over the years, that passion has grown through the homes we have lived in and lovingly decorated, including our home in the Cotswolds. Each project has allowed me to develop an eye for colour, texture, furniture and styling, always with the aim of creating interiors that feel timeless, comfortable and effortlessly elegant.

After a career in marketing and events, I spent the last decade focused on raising my family while creating homes that reflected the way we live. Throughout that time, friends and visitors often encouraged me to pursue my passion professionally, inspiring the creation of Claudia Silva Interiors.

Today, I help homeowners create beautiful, relaxed homes through thoughtful decorating, styling and carefully considered design choices. Whether refreshing a single room or bringing new life to an entire home, my goal is to create spaces that feel both practical and personal.

I believe that great homes do not always require major renovations. Sometimes, a fresh perspective, the right colours, carefully chosen furnishings and thoughtful styling can completely transform the way a home feels.
